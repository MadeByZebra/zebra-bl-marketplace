Privacy Policy.

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Review before public launch

We collect the information required to provide store accounts, verify purchases, and answer customer support questions. This page describes the features currently used by Zebra's Shop. We do not collect or store full payment card numbers on the website.

Account information

To manage access, the website stores your email address, account and session identifiers, verification information and purchase records. One-time sign-in codes are sent by email, expire after a limited time, and are not displayed to other users. Historical accounts may also have password hashes. Authentication sessions use HTTP-only cookies.

Orders and payment processing

Payments are processed through Stripe Checkout. Stripe handles card information and provides transaction details, status and receipt links needed to verify that a purchase is paid before issuing product access. The website stores purchase references and entitlement status, not payment card numbers. Stripe's own privacy practices apply to payment data processed by Stripe.

Chat and support

The Zebra Support chat runs on our own website. Messages are stored in our shop database so that our automated FAQ assistant can answer common questions and a human operator can review and reply when requested. A conversation token may be stored in your browser to reopen the same chat. A human-assistance request may generate an email notification through Brevo. Messages may be retained for support, security, and troubleshooting until no longer needed; you can request deletion using our support form. If you email us, your message is also handled by the relevant email providers. Never send full card details, passwords, private license keys, or sign-in codes in chat, email, or support requests.

Email delivery

We use a transactional email provider, currently Brevo, to send sign-in codes and order confirmations. Providers may process addresses and message-delivery metadata to deliver and troubleshoot these emails.

Technical information

Our website and infrastructure providers may process limited technical data such as IP addresses, request times, browser information and security logs. Our first-party chat uses local browser storage to remember a conversation, and the shop stores support messages in its database. Hosting, email, and payment providers may retain technical records under their own policies.

Your questions and requests

You can contact us about your account data, corrections, or deletion requests at zebrablshopofficial@gmail.com. Certain payment and transaction records may need to be kept for legal, security, fraud-prevention or bookkeeping reasons.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services and providers change. Customers should consult this page for the current version.
