SOLUTIONS / SELF SERVICE

Frequently asked questions.

Find quick answers first, then contact us if your issue isn't resolved.

How can I find my purchase or receipt?
Sign in with the same email address used during checkout at My Library. Your completed, verified orders appear in your order history. When a Stripe receipt is available, use the receipt option attached to that order.
I paid, but my item is missing. What should I do?
First check that you're signed into the purchasing email account and refresh My Library. If an order still doesn't appear, email Zebra Support with the product name and the date of purchase. Never send full card numbers, passwords, or one-time login codes. We can help check the payment.
My login email code isn't arriving.
Check Spam and Promotions and verify the spelling of your email address. Wait at least a minute before requesting another code; codes expire after 10 minutes. If none arrives, contact support with the email address you used.
Do STR and END Grinder support Class B or Class C?
No. The currently described STR and END Grinder tools are for Class A only. Class B and C are plans, not included in the current products. Don't purchase expecting access to future classes.
Can I get a refund if something doesn't work?
Contact Zebra Support by email with your order reference, product name, and a description of the problem. Requests are reviewed individually; refunds are offered when required by applicable law, including for products missing, faulty, or materially different from their descriptions. See the Terms & refunds page.
Will the tools always work after game updates?
No software can be guaranteed compatible with every future change in a third-party game or client. Read each product description and compatibility notes before purchase. The store is independent of Roblox and Boxing League, and using third-party tools may violate those platforms' rules.
Why does checkout say sandbox or unavailable?
The store has a restricted live checkout pilot for approved accounts. If an item says ON HOLD, it is not available to the public yet. Stripe Sandbox orders, if offered, are simulations; approved live orders use real money and are labeled accordingly.
Is ZFLAG available to download?
ZFLAG availability depends on release and installer verification. If its download isn't offered in your account, it has not been made available for purchase or delivery yet. Review the ZFLAG page for the current status.
Can I use a paid script on my Roblox alt accounts?
Yes, the license belongs to your purchasing BL Scripts account rather than a single Roblox username. Use your newest personal loadstring on the approved installation. If the loader asks for an activation code, sign in to My Library and approve it there. Replacing your key revokes old commands. A shared key does not authorize another person's installation.
Is chat support available around the clock?
You can leave a message in the support chat or email any time. Automated responses are limited and may not resolve every issue; replies from a human are not guaranteed immediately. For an unresolved order, email Zebra Support and include a reachable reply address without sharing sensitive payment details.
